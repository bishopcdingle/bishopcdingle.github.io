A life defined by the Word of God

Bishop Dr. Charles W. Dingle has devoted over 53 years to pastoral ministry, biblical scholarship, and theological education. His journey has been forged not only in the halls of scholarship but in the crucible of ministry and in intimate moments of spiritual wrestling with the Word of God.

"The soul lies at the absolute center of our identity, intricately woven into our relationship with the Creator and our fellow man."

Through fervent prayer, exhaustive study, and deep personal experience, Bishop Dingle has reached a non-negotiable conviction that the Church urgently needs to hear.

  • Founder, True Standard Christian Theological Seminary, Tarboro, North Carolina
  • Author of Man Is Soul; Soul Is Man and The Heart Intruder: A Collection of Poetry, in English and Spanish
  • 40+ years of theological reflection on the nature of the soul

True Standard Christian Theological Seminary

The seminary was founded by Bishop Dr. Charles W. Dingle as an institution committed to one standard: the uncompromising truth of the Word of God.

Located in Tarboro, North Carolina, it offers undergraduate and graduate programs, with courses that range from Bible studies and homiletics to philosophy, equipping the next generation of spiritual leaders.
